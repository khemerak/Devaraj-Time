# Privacy notice template — company review required

Replace bracketed items and obtain jurisdiction-specific employment/privacy advice before presenting this notice to employees. This template does not establish compliance with Cambodia or any other jurisdiction.

**Controller:** [Company name, address, contact and privacy contact].

**Purpose:** We use Devaraj Time to record attendance, review work-time discrepancies and administer approved leave. We collect account identity, company membership/assignment, employee code, attendance times/actions, applied shift-policy snapshots, request/decision reasons and audit history.

**Location:** Only when you explicitly initiate attendance at a workplace requiring a geofence do we request a fresh browser position, reported accuracy and measurement timestamp. We use these values to compare with that workplace's configured boundary. We do not continuously track your location. Browser geolocation and printed QR codes can be falsified and are evidence, not conclusive proof of presence or misconduct.

**Choice and exceptions:** If camera/location access is denied or unavailable, explain the issue through an attendance correction request for review. An unrecorded or rejected scan does not automatically establish absence. Proposed correction times remain distinguishable from original server-recorded attendance.

**Access:** You can view your own records and original events. Authorized company administrators/HR and assigned department managers can view or review records within their roles. Database operators and deployment providers have technical access according to [company processing agreements].

**Retention:** Private location evidence is retained for [configured days] and erased by the scheduled retention job. Clock records, account data, audit history, leave/correction reasons and backups follow [company policy and lawful retention schedule]. The current application has no self-service legal-erasure or account-retention administration tool; contact [privacy contact].

**Employee rights and questions:** Contact [contact] for access, correction, dispute, deletion or other applicable requests. Available rights and lawful grounds depend on [jurisdiction]. We will consider unresolved disputes before consequential employment/pay decisions under [company procedure].

**Security:** Authentication, role and tenant access controls and protected audit history are implemented, but no system guarantees absence of breaches or spoofing. [Company] is responsible for deployment security, access reviews, backups, retention scheduling and incident-response procedures.

**Legal review items:** lawful basis and workplace monitoring rules; employee notice/consultation; retention obligations; access/deletion rights; wage/overtime/leave law; evidential standards; international transfers; processor/subprocessor agreements; incident reporting; accessibility and non-smartphone exception processes.
